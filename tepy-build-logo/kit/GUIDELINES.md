# Tepy Build Co., Ltd. — Brand Guidelines

## 1. The logo
**Idea:** A blue design slab and an orange build column lock together into one T. The shape shows Tepy's design-build offer.

| Version | File | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal (primary) | `master/tepy-horizontal.svg` | Letterhead, bid covers, proposals, website header, email |
| Stacked | `master/tepy-stacked.svg` | Site signboards, hoardings, uniforms, vehicle doors |
| Symbol | `master/tepy-symbol.svg` | Profile pictures, hard hats, stamps, favicon |
| Symbol, small | `master/tepy-symbol-small.svg` | Anything under 48 px (the gap is wider so it doesn't close) |
| Wordmark | `master/tepy-wordmark.svg` | When the symbol is already shown nearby |
| Reversed (`-reversed`) | `master/*-reversed.svg` | On navy or dark backgrounds (the slab and "TEPY" turn white) |

## 2. Clear space and minimum size
- **Clear space:** keep a space at least as wide as the orange column's width around the logo on all sides.
- **Horizontal:** at least 220 px wide on screen and 45 mm in print. Below that the tagline can't be read, so use the symbol.
- **Symbol:** at least 16 px on screen (use the `-small` file) and 8 mm in print.

## 3. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (starting values) | Pantone (nearest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tepy Navy | `#0B3C6F` | 11 60 111 | 90 46 0 56 | 7694 C |
| Tepy Orange | `#F37021` | 243 112 33 | 0 54 86 5 | 1585 C |
| Slate (tagline text) | `#4A5B6E` | 74 91 110 | 33 17 0 57 | — |

- The CMYK and Pantone values are starting points: confirm them on a printed proof before large runs such as signboards or uniforms.
- **One-colour versions** (black, white, navy) are in `digital/`. Use them for stamps, engraving, embroidery and fax.

## 4. Typography
- **Logo letters:** Montserrat ExtraBold, outlined in the logo files. Never retype the logo.
- **Tagline:** Montserrat SemiBold.
- **Documents:** Montserrat or Arial for headings, Arial or Calibri for body text.
- Montserrat is free under the SIL Open Font License from Google Fonts.

## 6. Don'ts
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or gradients.
- Don't swap the two colours: the slab is always navy (or white on dark) and the column always orange.
- Don't close the gap between the slab and the column.
- Don't place the logo on busy site photos without a white or navy panel behind it.
